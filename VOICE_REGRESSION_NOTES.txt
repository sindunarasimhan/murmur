User-reported behavior
- Premature channel closing improved in the latest phone test.
- Detail-page input remains unreliable via mascot tap and also reportedly via
  wake phrase. Intended: "play from the beginning". Displayed: "just let beginning".
  Response asked which podcast to play instead of helping within the episode.

Established evidence, not a root-cause claim
- Microphone PCM conversion, silence threshold, eight-second forced commits,
  provider transcription configuration, and voice processing were not changed
  by the isolated reconnect repair. The current native checkpoint makes future
  comparisons possible; it is not a verified last-known-good phone build.
- Tap pauses playback but has no audio-buffer reset or pending-transcript
  boundary. Contamination by earlier audio is a hypothesis requiring reproduction.
- The catalog clarification helper defaults to asking for a show/guest/topic,
  even when a current episode is supplied and the input is unintelligible.
- A separate stored request, "Move to like ten minutes", was received correctly
  but rejected downstream. Three live probes reproduced seek-mode uncertainty,
  while action and time-target judgments were strong. This is not accent evidence.
- Server logs contain transcript lengths, not the original microphone recording;
  they cannot establish what audio reached transcription for the reported phrase.

Engineering lessons and acceptance gates
- Separate capture, segmentation, transcription, routing, and execution failures.
  Do not label them all accent problems or infer a cause from empty transcripts.
- Do not hardcode misheard phrases or relax confidence thresholds to hide errors.
- Establish a last-known-good build and exact device build before comparison.
- Preserve the user's natural wording, pauses, corrections, and pace in evaluation.
  Generated speech is a useful regression check, not proof of accent recognition.
- Exercise home and detail separately: tap, wake phrase, before/after playback,
  repeated turns, renewal, interruption, pause/resume, and background/foreground.
- Introduce only a reproduced, scoped correction. Do not bundle unrelated cleanup.
- Record failing evidence before changing behavior; distinguish source changes,
  simulator test results, installed phone build, and user-confirmed quality.
