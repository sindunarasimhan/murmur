Time seek extension — 2026-10-08

Scope: backend time interpretation only. No native UI, microphone, player,
ad-boundary, intro-boundary, or session lifecycle implementation changes.

Time amounts are parsed from source text (seconds/minutes/hours, decimal and
spoken numbers, fractions, compound durations, timestamps). Existing exact
transport shortcuts remain; other wording uses Jev to select a candidate amount
and relative forward/backward or absolute mode. No ten-minute limit. Existing
playbackAction clamps to episode bounds; ListeningService preserves prior play
state through resumeAfterAction.

Failure reproduced: "Please move back two minutes." produced no time candidate
because a terminal full stop was rejected by the candidate boundary. Jev was
therefore never offered a seek action. Fixed punctuation handling with a direct
regression; thresholds unchanged.
